# Northline Construction Operations

## The problem

Construction teams often run critical operations through paper forms and disconnected Excel files. That creates delayed visibility into budget overruns, inaccurate labor tracking, and slow issue resolution. By the time a manager sees the true cost of a project or learns about a site risk, the opportunity to correct it may already be gone.

## The solution

Northline is a role-based construction operations platform that gives the team one live view of the build:

- **Attendance:** workers and supervisors record check-in and check-out against assigned projects.
- **Materials:** site teams track inventory and material movements by project.
- **Finance:** accountants record expenses and compare project budgets with actual spend.
- **Safety issues:** supervisors report, prioritize, and resolve construction issues with optional photos.
- **Dashboards:** leadership sees project status, spend, attendance, and open risks in one overview.

## Before and after

| Before | After Northline |
| --- | --- |
| A budget overrun is discovered weeks later in a spreadsheet. | The overrun is visible the same day in the Finance view. |
| Attendance sheets are reconciled manually. | Project-scoped attendance is recorded digitally. |
| A site issue waits in a WhatsApp thread. | Severity, status, owner, and history are visible to the team. |
| Every role sees the same sensitive information. | RBAC gives each person the right operational view. |

## Proposed next step

Start with a free or discounted pilot on one active project. In one short implementation, Northline can establish a live baseline for labor, cost, materials, and safety—and give the project team a measurable path away from paper and spreadsheet delays.
